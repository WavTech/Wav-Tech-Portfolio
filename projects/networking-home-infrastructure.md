# Residential Networking & Home Infrastructure

## Overview

Wav Tech has completed residential networking and infrastructure projects focused on reliable connectivity, gaming performance, VPN access, clean equipment organization, and expandable home-network design.

## Mesh Wi-Fi & VPN Gaming Networks

Configured mesh-based residential networks for gaming-focused environments requiring stronger wireless coverage and VPN-enabled connectivity.

### Work Performed

- Evaluated coverage and connectivity requirements
- Installed and configured mesh Wi-Fi equipment
- Configured local addressing and DHCP behavior
- Connected gaming systems and endpoints
- Configured VPN functionality
- Tested wired and wireless connectivity
- Verified Internet access throughout the environment
- Troubleshot latency and connectivity issues
- Optimized equipment placement

### Skills Demonstrated

`Mesh Wi-Fi` · `VPN` · `DHCP` · `DNS` · `TCP/IP` · `Ethernet` · `Wireless Networking` · `Gaming Network Optimization`

## Compact 10-Inch Home Network Racks

Designed and installed small 10-inch rack environments for residential clients who wanted networking equipment centralized and organized rather than spread across shelves or loose cabling.

### Typical Scope

- Evaluate rack and equipment requirements
- Plan compact rack layout
- Install 10-inch rack
- Mount compatible network equipment
- Organize Ethernet connections
- Manage patch cables
- Organize power connectivity
- Connect network devices
- Test network links after installation

### Typical Equipment

- Router/firewall
- Ethernet switch
- Patch panel
- Mesh/network equipment
- Power distribution
- Ethernet patch cabling
