# Studio & Creative Technology

## Overview

Wav Tech has supported music and creative environments with hands-on technical setup and troubleshooting.

## Studio Equipment Setup

Project work has included assisting with studio equipment installation, connectivity, and configuration.

### Work Performed

- Equipment placement and setup
- Connecting audio and computing equipment
- Peripheral integration
- Cable organization
- Device configuration
- Basic signal-path troubleshooting
- Computer-to-studio-device connectivity
- General technical troubleshooting
- Post-setup testing

## Technical Focus

The work is positioned as technology support rather than production or artistic services. The emphasis is on making the hardware and connected systems function reliably for the client’s intended workflow.

## Skills Demonstrated

`Hardware Setup` · `Peripheral Integration` · `Cable Management` · `Device Configuration` · `Technical Troubleshooting` · `Studio Technology`
